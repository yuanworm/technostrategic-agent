# RE:AI — BGM Brief (Singtel DICo)

## Client & Product
- **Client:** Singtel DICo
- **Product:** RE:AI — AI/GPUaaS (GPU-as-a-Service, AI infrastructure)
- **Target market:** Japan

## BGM Workstreams (RE:AI)

### Workstream 1: Build buying group intelligence
**AI role:**
- Monitor 30 strategic logos and track buying intent/signals: company AI priorities, expansion,
  investments, leadership movements, Japan regulations, relationship adjacency, ecosystem affiliations
- Map buying groups — names, roles, key contacts (emails/numbers)
- 1-page account summaries & suggested talking points >> weekly scans and triggers when there are changes

**Human role (DICo):**
- Filters noise; validates account summaries, assesses which account matters, who matters, why it matters
- Identifies actionable top accounts with readiness and propensity for AI/GPUaaS needs

**Success indicators:**
- 5–10 relevant stakeholders across 2 or more functions per account
- Contact database improvements

### Workstream 2: Content and comms
**AI role:**
- Relevant narratives by accounts
- Content drafts and adaptations in Japanese aligned to RE:AI content pillars (visual, copy, etc)

**Human role (DICo Marketing):**
- Editorial control, refinement and judgment
- Future iterations

**Success indicators:**
- Content templates
- Production agility and speed
